    Questions

    Do I need to provide water or power?

    No. We carry our own water tank and generator, so we can work in a driveway, parking lot, or street spot.

    Can you detail at an apartment or my job?

    Yes, as long as there's room around the car and the property allows it. Leave any gate codes or parking notes in the booking form.

    What if it rains?

    We'll text you to move your booking to the next open slot at no charge.

    How do I pay?

    After the job, by card, Zelle, or cash. Nothing is charged when you book.

    How long does a detail take?

    From about an hour for an Express wash up to a full day for Ceramic. Each package lists its time above.
